**BEBLaDII**: Phase 3 — Continuous Spherical Diffusion Core with global t.

In my last post, I outlined the concept of continuous text diffusion on a hypersphere. And Phase 3 delivers this milestone: the 40-layer core is trained, stabilized, and actively denoising.

Getting here required surviving a cascade of numerical and architectural crises. Here is what Phase 3 was about, how we made it work, and the results.

🎯 **The Objective**
Train a 40-layer DUS Backbone (ModernBERT-large scaled via Depth Up-Scaling) to perform continuous denoising directly on the S^(1023) hyperspherical manifold. Given a noised latent vector at continuous time t in [0, 1], the model must iteratively recover the clean original semantic vector x_0. A defining feature of this phase was applying a uniform noise level across the entire canvas.

🛠️ **The Battles & Breakthroughs**
We initially tried shortcuts: confidence embeddings, teacher distillation, and steep non-linear penalties. They backfired. We hit bfloat16 gradient collapse, Pre-LN norm explosions, and AdaLN residual stream multiplication bugs that broke identity mapping.

The breakthrough came from stripping away crutches and moving to canonical, mathematically sound continuous diffusion:
• **Strict float32 Precision**: Eliminated the rounding and underflow issues of bfloat16 on the hypersphere.
• **Cosine Schedule & SLERP**: Full continuous noise range t in [0, 1] using Spherical Linear Interpolation for noise injection.
• **DiT-Style Zero-Init AdaLN**: Time conditioning integrated inside EVERY ModernBERT block.
• **t-Dependent Output Gate**: Structurally guarantees identity mapping at t=0.
• **Self-Conditioning**: Re-enabled with Xavier initialization to give the core a "second glance" at its own preliminary x_0 prediction during denoising.

⚡ **The Results**
Using our Phase 2 grammatical decoder to inspect denoising trajectories step-by-step:
✓ **Low noise (t <= 0.3)**: Near-instant semantic recovery with Cosine Similarity > 0.98.
✓ **Medium noise (t = 0.4 – 0.6)**: Robust semantic healing. Even when heavy noise pushes the trajectory toward neighboring concepts (e.g., predicting "bread fox" instead of "brown fox"), it converges smoothly back to the exact target sentence ("The quick brown fox...").
✓ **High / Pure noise (t >= 0.8)**: Generates grammatically coherent token attractors — the expected, mathematically correct unconditional behavior without prompt guidance.

Phase 3 proved that continuous diffusion on a spherical manifold is not just viable, but robust. The foundation is set.

Next in line is Phase 4 — diffusion with per-token noise levels, training the model to leverage confident context on the canvas to denoise the remaining tokens.
