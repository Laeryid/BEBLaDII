𝐍𝐞𝐰 𝐚𝐠𝐞𝐧𝐭 𝐬𝐤𝐢𝐥𝐥 — 𝐓𝐏𝐔 𝐬𝐜𝐫𝐢𝐩𝐭 𝐜𝐫𝐚𝐟𝐭𝐢𝐧𝐠.

For the past couple of weeks, Google servers have been choking, and Kaggle compute queues have been brutal. A script waits 5 to 20 hours (no kidding) just to start running.

Debugging TPU code under these conditions is pure pain. Over two weeks, I burned through 15 queued runs and still failed to eliminate all the errors before actual training could start. What made it even more chaotic: I was running GPU experiments in parallel, finding architectural issues, and altering the model architecture on the fly.

Once my GPU quotas finally ran out, I had a moment to step back and realize the obvious: a substantial portion of TPU and XLA errors can easily be caught locally.

So I put together a dedicated agent skill for crafting and auditing Google TPU training scripts.

What is included:
🛠️ 𝐏𝐲𝐓𝐨𝐫𝐜𝐡/𝐗𝐋𝐀 𝐂𝐡𝐞𝐜𝐤𝐥𝐢𝐬𝐭: Traps around dynamic shapes, SPMD/FSDP sharding, and graph compilation traps.
⚡ 𝐀𝐒𝐓 & 𝐒𝐭𝐚𝐭𝐢𝐜 𝐀𝐧𝐚𝐥𝐲𝐬𝐢𝐬: Automated checks for host-device syncs (.item(), .cpu()), unsupported operations, and graph breaks.
✓ 𝐌𝐞𝐦𝐨𝐫𝐲 & 𝐎𝐎𝐌 𝐕𝐚𝐥𝐢𝐝𝐚𝐭𝐢𝐨𝐧: Rough estimation of activation and tensor overhead to catch OOM crashes before scheduling.

I don't see any point in making a separate repository for it, at least for now. If you're dealing with TPU queues and want to avoid burning hours on basic crashes, you can find the skill here:

https://github.com/Laeryid/BEBLaDII/tree/main/.agents/skills/tpu-script-crafting
