**BEBLaDII**: Phase 4 — Per-Token Individual Noising & Semantic Skepticism.

From the start, I envisioned text diffusion differently from how AI vibe-coded it back in Phase 3.

In Phase 3, we stabilized canonical spherical diffusion. There, the noise level was uniform across the entire canvas and decayed synchronously for all tokens.

What I actually wanted was for the model to first sketch out key concepts and anchor words, and only then construct the logical bridges between them — gradually refining the response from a coarse outline into fine details.

To achieve this, I decoupled noise into two levels:
• **Global noise** (t_global), defining the generation phase across the canvas.
• **Local noise** (t_local), defining the uncertainty and corruption of each individual token.

Here is what went into making this work:

1. **Reworked AdaLN**: The time-conditioning layer was overhauled to inject information about both the global phase and per-token local noise into every transformer block.

2. **Dynamic Dataset Noising**: The noise scheduling logic was upgraded so that the per-token noise formula dynamically adapts across each stage of global noise.

3. **'Liar' Tokens & Semantic Skepticism**: Introduced artificially deceptive tokens with falsely reported low noise. If a token claims high certainty but contradicts the surrounding canvas, the model must prioritize global semantic coherence. Through bidirectional attention, the model learns to critically audit context and discard impostors.

Plus plenty of low-level technical plumbing under the hood.

The results at checkpoint step_85995:
✓ Clean anchor confidence at t_global = 0.5: 0.9995
✓ Detection & scrubbing of falsely confident tokens: 0.9996
✓ Cosine similarity after 25 Slerp steps from 100% noise: 0.84–0.91

Spot the catch in this approach?
Noise levels during training are calculated because we already know the ground-truth clean state — but during real inference, that clean state is unknown!
