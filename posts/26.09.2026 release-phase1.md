**BEBLaDII**: Phase 1 — Building a spherical latent space. 🚀
Training diffusion models on text is a tough challenge. Text is discrete, but diffusion requires a continuous manifold.

Otherwise, adding noise generates nonsense in the "dead zones" of the space. That's why I decided to work in the space of meanings, not words. The goal of Phase 1 was to build a reliable bridge between discrete tokens (Qwen) and a continuous diffusion core using a custom mu-VAE layer.

**The Pivot** 🔄
In Plan A, I tried using a projector to recover the original token space directly from the deep latent state after 40 Transformer layers. The result was fatal: **Garbage Collapse**. The topology was so tangled that recursive inference lost initial semantics and collapsed into dense garbage hubs. I realized the diffusion core itself must learn to work with meanings; a small projector wouldn't suffice.

**What kind of space do we have now?** 🌌
I switched to a strictly spherical, continuous, and isotropic latent space. The biggest insight: replacing `LayerNorm` (which normalizes variance but doesn't guarantee unit norm) with a strict mathematical L2 normalization.

This gave the space the shape of a perfect sphere, solving issues with classic Euclidean spaces like passing near zero where vectors hold no meaning. Now, the neural network doesn't have to struggle with amplitudes — vectors are projected back to the sphere, and the diffusion core focuses purely on angles.

**Loss Function: The topology recipe** 🛠️
To compress complex semantics into a sphere without losing meaning, we used a hybrid loss:

✓ **Reconstruction Loss**: Basic cross-entropy to control meaning recovery via a frozen Qwen decoder with a tiny adapter trained alongside the encoder.
✓ **Variance-Only KL Divergence**: Full KL-loss caused gradient conflicts on the hypersphere. We kept only the variance penalty to maintain stochasticity and "blurred meanings," letting cluster centers move freely.
✓ **Covariance Loss**: Penalizes non-diagonal covariance matrix elements. Forces the use of all orthogonal dimensions and prevents collapsing into a "pancake."
✓ **Uniformity Loss**: Replaced the problematic InfoNCE, uniformly distributing tokens across the hypersphere and completely eliminating the hubness problem.

**The Results** ⚡
The checkpoint at 20k steps showed benchmark results:

- **Perfect sphericity**, ensured by normalization math.
- **True semantic compression**: VAE doesn't memorize tokens, it grasps concepts. During decoding, it easily swaps words for synonyms (e.g., `laws` to `principles`), keeping the reasoning intact.
- **Smooth interpolation**: SLERP tests show seamless transitions between concepts with no "voids" or garbage generations.
- **Good isotropy**: ~330 effective dimensions out of 1024.

The foundation is poured. The space is continuous and full of meaning. Moving on to training the Latent Diffusion Backbone itself.
